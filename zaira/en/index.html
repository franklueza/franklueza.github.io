Do you need more time than a 50-minute session allows?

EMDR Intensives are longer, focused therapy sessions, typically lasting three to five hours. They are designed for people who want dedicated, uninterrupted time to work through a specific concern without having to divide the process across several weekly sessions.

Virtual EMDR Intensives are available to adults anywhere in Texas. Each intensive includes thoughtful preparation, a plan tailored to your needs, and time at the end to feel grounded, close the session calmly, and reflect on the work.

Why work with me?

I believe healing happens within a relationship where you can feel safe, understood, respected, accepted, and genuinely supported without fear of judgment. Therapy is not only about the approach used; it is also about having a space where you can show up as you are and feel comfortable sharing what may be difficult to put into words.

I work with you the way I would want someone to work with me: with patience, curiosity, and genuine interest in who you are as a person, not only in your symptoms. Our work goes beyond giving you a place to vent. I use trauma-informed approaches that consider the body and nervous system, and, if you’re open to it, I’ll gently challenge you when something may be worth exploring more deeply.

My approach brings together EMDR, attachment- and emotion-focused therapy, nervous system regulation, mindfulness, and values-based work. We choose and adapt these tools based on what helps you, rather than following a fixed formula.
